Henge's pre-alpha is now available for testing. You can create a character, explore Hearthmoor and nearby areas, gather wood, ore, and fish, then try cooking, smithing, and the first quests. One quest leads to the Wolf form, and you can meet and trade with other players in the shared world.

This is an early build. We're especially interested in reports on performance, controls, and anything confusing in your first hours of play.
